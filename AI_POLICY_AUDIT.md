# Generative-AI policy audit

## Official sources checked

- Journal of Geodesy submission guidelines:
  <https://link.springer.com/journal/190/submission-guidelines>
- Springer Nature AI guidance for researchers, editors, and reviewers:
  <https://group.springernature.com/us/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-researchers-editors-reviewers>

Persisted page snapshots and checksums are recorded in
`data/data_acquisition_log.csv`.

## Policy requirements applied

The guidance states that an AI system cannot be an author, the human author
remains accountable, and generative-AI use must be transparently declared.
The current Springer Nature guidance requests the tool version, usage dates,
prompts, and extent of contribution. Journal guidance requires LLM use to be
documented in the Methods section or an equivalent manuscript section.

## Implemented disclosure

The manuscript declarations, declarations document, and standalone
`AI_DISCLOSURE.txt` identify:

- Devin as a Cognition web service and state that no user-visible version
  identifier was available;
- use dates of 24–25 September 2026;
- the purposes of the author prompts;
- assistance with literature metadata, Python implementation and checks,
  deterministic simulation replication, display/document generation,
  numerical reconciliation, and language revision;
- that no empirical source data or experimental images were generated by AI;
- that displayed results were generated by disclosed code from persisted
  inputs and configurations; and
- the author's verification, scientific decision-making, accountability, and
  final responsibility.

## Verdict

The disclosure is transparent and consistent with the checked Journal of
Geodesy and Springer Nature guidance.
