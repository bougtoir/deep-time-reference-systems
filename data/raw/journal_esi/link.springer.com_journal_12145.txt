Overview

Earth Science Informatics [ESIN] aims for rapid publication of high-quality, current, cutting-edge, and provocative scientific work in the area of Earth Science Informatics as it relates to Earth systems science and space science. Appropriate subject matter include:

Articles on the application of formal and computational methods, computational Earth science, spatial and temporal analyses, and all aspects of computer applications to the Earth system.
Research, methodology, and software articles.
Editorials, comments, and book and software reviews.
Review articles of relevant findings, topics, and methodologies.
