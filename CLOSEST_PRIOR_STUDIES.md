# Closest prior studies (internal — not for submission package)

1. Sillard & Boucher (2001), JoG 75:63–73 — reviews algebraic datum constraints in
   terrestrial frames. Adds nothing about post-metadata-loss identifiability; our audit is
   downstream of this. Differentiation: "we ask which relations remain identifiable after
   the realization fails, not how constraints select a realization."
2. Kotsakis & Chatzinikos (2017), JoG 91:627–652 — rank-defect / proper singularity in
   normal equations. Overlap risk: the "rank deficiency" vocabulary; our residual-invariance
   group is an observational equivalence over archived observables, not a normal-equation
   null space.
3. Chatzinikos & Dermanis (2017), JoG 91:375–396 — coordinate-invariant deforming-network
   model; closest "invariance" precedent. Differentiation: their invariance concerns the
   adjustment model; ours concerns archived relational claims.
4. Dokmanić et al. (2015), IEEE SPM 32(6) — EDM theory; supplies the O(3) reconstruction
   baseline. We add the directional-anchor hierarchy on top of their uniqueness result.
5. Liberti et al. (2014), SIAM Review 56(1) — distance-geometry identifiability survey.
   Same overlap and differentiation as (4).
6. Asimow & Roth (1978, 1979) — generic rigidity; supplies the 3n−6 rank diagnostic only.
7. Bevis & Brown (2014), JoG 88 — trajectory models / time-dependent frames; supports the
   epoch requirement, not the identifiability hierarchy.
8. Altamimi et al. (2023), JoG 97 — ITRF2020; data source and realization exemplar.
9. Torsvik et al. (2008), Torsvik & Cocks (2019) — absolute plate frames / paleolongitude
   model dependence; motivates but does not overlap the archival-identifiability claim.
10. ISO 19111:2019 / IERS TN36 — terminology basis (CRS, datum, realization, epoch).
