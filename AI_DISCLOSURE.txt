Generative AI disclosure

During preparation of this work, the author used the Devin web service by Cognition (no user-visible version identifier; used in September and November 2026). Prompts requested literature-metadata verification, Python implementation and checking, simulation and benchmark execution, figure/table/document generation, numerical reconciliation and language revision. The tool did not supply empirical source data or generate experimental images; all numerical results and display items were regenerated by the disclosed code from persisted inputs and configurations. The author reviewed and verified the code, outputs, references, figures and text, made the scientific and editorial decisions, and takes full responsibility for the content. This use is also described in the Methods section (Sect. 4, Use of AI tools).
