# Final minor-revision review

## 1. Mathematical geodesy review

**Verdict: pass.**

The manuscript now defines the residual invariance group as the transformations
\(Q\) satisfying \(F(QX)=F(X)\) for the retained observable map \(F\). It
explicitly distinguishes this observational equivalence group from the
pointwise stabilizer of a generic realized configuration.

Under the stated assumptions—labeled generic points, removed translation,
known metric scale, and Euclidean chord distance—the hierarchy

\[
O(3)\rightarrow O(2)\rightarrow SO(2)\rightarrow\{I\}
\]

is correct. A directed physical axis leaves the orthogonal transformations
fixing that axis, a physically recoverable chiral witness excludes the
improper branch, and an independent directed meridian removes the remaining
axial rotation. Datum and gauge terminology is reserved for established
geodetic realization and rigidity contexts. Surface-geodesic distance,
scale loss, deformation, and non-generic configurations are kept outside the
proved claim.

## 2. Rigidity-theory review

**Verdict: pass.**

The incomplete-network experiment tests generic three-dimensional frameworks
with numerical rigidity-matrix rank \(3n-6\), after the six infinitesimal
Euclidean gauge modes. It reports 2,000 trials per \((n,p)\) cell, Wilson 95%
intervals, and excess nullity.

The text consistently calls the result local rigidity. Table 3 reports the
first tested probabilities whose observed frequencies meet 0.50 and 0.95; it
does not label them universal or asymptotic thresholds. The manuscript states
that local rank does not prove global rigidity, graph completion, or global
uniqueness.
